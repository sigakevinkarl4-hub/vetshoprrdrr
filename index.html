More About Vetshoppe

Vetshoppe was built with a simple mission: to make pet adoption and veterinary care seamless, transparent, and accessible for everyone. Our platform bridges the gap between animal shelters, veterinary clinics, and loving families.

Our Mission

We believe every pet deserves a loving home and proper medical care. By digitizing adoption workflows and health records, we reduce friction in the adoption process and ensure no pet's medical history is ever lost.

Key Features

  • Real-time Pet Profiles — Detailed info on breed, age, vaccination status, and availability.
  • One-Click Adoption — Clients can adopt instantly and track their new companion.
  • Veterinary Records — Complete diagnosis, treatment, and vet history for every pet.
  • QR Code Lookup — Scan any pet's unique QR to instantly pull up their profile.
  • Admin Dashboard — Full CRUD control over pets and records with export capabilities.
  • Multi-Role Access — Separate portals for admins and clients with tailored experiences.
  • Adoption Pricing — Clear, upfront fees for every pet — no surprises.
  • Analytics Dashboard — Visualize adoption trends and shelter statistics.
  • Favorites & Wishlist — Save pets you're interested in for later.
  • Appointment Booking — Schedule vet visits for your pets.
  • Notification Center — Stay updated on adoptions and records.
  • Activity Log — Track all admin actions for accountability.

Who It's For

Shelter Admins

Manage pet inventory, track adoptions, set pricing, and maintain comprehensive veterinary records — all from one intuitive dashboard.

Adopting Families

Browse available pets, view their health history and adoption fees, and adopt with confidence knowing all medical info is verified.

Veterinarians

Access and update pet medical records, ensuring continuity of care across different clinics and visits.

Why Choose Vetshoppe?

Unlike traditional paper-based systems, Vetshoppe centralizes everything in one secure digital hub. Our platform reduces adoption processing time, eliminates lost records, and provides full transparency to both adopters and shelter staff — including clear adoption pricing for every pet.

Install Vetshoppe App

The Vetshoppe mobile app isn't quite ready yet. Here's how you can get the app on your device:

Android (APK)

  1. Download the Vetshoppe.apk file
  2. Open Settings → Security
  3. Enable "Install from Unknown Sources"
  4. Open the APK and tap Install
  5. Launch Vetshoppe from your app drawer 🎉

iOS Users

Use Add to Home Screen in Safari for a native-app experience. iOS support is coming soon!

Install as PWA (Works Now!)

Chrome (Android): Menu (⋮) → "Install app"
Safari (iOS): Share → "Add to Home Screen"
Desktop (Chrome/Edge): Install icon in the address bar
